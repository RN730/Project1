The World of Acheronia

Acheronia is a realm shrouded in mystery and ancient magic, where foreigneldritch horrors dwell beneath the surface and vie for control against the native gods. A world of dark forests, crumbling ruins, and forgotten gods, where the line between reality and nightmare blurs.

Surving in this land not only requires the protection of enigmatic beings but also personal strength and usefulness. Without value, your life is disposable to the whims of those above. You better pray that the god that watches over your land is one of benevelonce and love.

World Overview

  • Geography: Vast continents with diverse biomes from frozen tundras to volcanic wastelands. In the image above is just one of those continents. Its name is Morgath. There are many more continets larger and smaller than it but our story begins here.
  • Magic System: Mana runs like an ocean acroos the continets of this world. Ancient eldritch powers also flow through hidden channels, granting power to those who dare to wield it. Saints, priests and holy knights can also wield divine abilites granted by thier patron god.
  • History: Thousands of years of civilization has passed, marked by the rise and fall of empires. There are eras alos fogotten by history as there are gaps within the available books. There exists groups that have the sole purpose of digging out what happened during these shrouded eras.
  • Current Era: Age of Uncertainty, where old alliances crumble and new threats emerge. Many of the hidden powers on the continents across the world are abrubtly awakening. This includes cults that worship eldritch gods and power hungry noble and merchant alliances. Its as if they have sensed an opportunity others have not.

Notable Characters

Evanescia Deluna

The Enchantress of Coiled Hearts

Evanescia Deluna is a young noblewoman of Velmoor who suffered from an unidentified, chronic illness that rendered her frail yet possessed of a striking, luminous beauty. Following her death, which defied the healing efforts of the church, she was resurrected by her lover, Razazel Cardinus, who sacrificed his own heart to restore her. Her return to life marks a significant transformation, leaving her with crimson eyes and the permanent presence of two distinct heartbeats within her chest.

Evanescia possesses exceptional mental capabilities. Before the birth of her brother, she was the only child and heir to the Deluna Dukedom. She had studied in the various lands bordering her kingdom and was known as agenius when it came to politics and diplomacy.

Her body however could not keep up with her mind. During her teenage years, she became severly ill with some disease that was slowly draning her life away from her. The church of Sol, whose holy water could even cure plague and bring back limbs was useless agaisnt this condition, even after they poured her multiple bottles of the stuff.

Only after her beloved Razazel, sacrificed his own heart to resurrect her did she regain her health and gained special abilities.

Corvyn Sors Rejicio

The Oracle of Death

Gifted with visions of deadly future that have yet to come, Corvyn saves everyone except those he is not meant to save. Plauged by constant nightmares from the future, his only purpose is to save others. But can he save himself from what is to come?

Corvyn was born a commoner, the son of a Velmoor dockworker, and grew up without significant family connections. His prophetic abilities manifested early in his life, beginning when he was only five years old. After predicting a neighbor’s daughter's accident, he learned quickly that his visions were not meant for open discussion. He chose to live quietly to protect his freedom from the Church of the Undying Sun, which often treats prophets as mere instruments.

Throughout his life, Corvyn has used his foresight with extreme caution to aid authorities while maintaining his anonymity. He recently attained the rank of baron after successfully warning Lord-Governor Ashwick about a massive flood in the eastern granary district. Despite these official services, he has consistently avoided becoming an instrument of the Church, which demands control over all prophetic figures. He prefers the life of a person with agency rather than serving as a tool for religious institutions.

He was even prasied by [Redacted] Cardinus for his ability as this was teh first time that man had seen such a special talent actually be used for good. It was only because of his ability that [Redacted] spared everyone who saw Evanescia's resurrection.

Razazel Cardinus

The Blade of Dawn

Razazel Cardinus is an enigmatic and formidable figure of supernatural origin who possesses an imposing physical stature, standing over seven and a half feet tall with a broad, powerful build. He is identified by his soaked, black hair, bare chest, and striking black eyes marked by three white lines that resemble cracks. Driven by profound, singular devotion to Evanescia Deluna, Razazel was willing to sacrifice his own heart—which he possessed in triplicate—to resurrect her after conventional medicine failed.

Razazel's strength is legendary, honed through decades of brutal combat on some of the harshest locations in the world. His greastword has never known defeat, and his physical prowess is unmatched except for certain mosntrous beings who train like monsters.

His combat skills are unparalleled, having mastered every form of greatsword warfare known to man. He can defeat entire armies single-handedly when the situation demands it. He did all this just so he could prove to his parents that he was capable of handling himself, that he didnt need their coddling.

He also has access to eldritch powers though he rarely uses them, as he believes his personal strength is most important. [Redacted] Cardinus is his elder brother, one of his 6 older siblings as he is the 7th and the youngest.

Major Locations

The Velmoor Kingdom

Jointly Governed by The Justicar Royal Family and Its Vasals

A once-great realm now struggling to maintain its borders against external threats and internal corruption. Known for its holy knights of the Sol Church and ancient ties to the ascended true god Sol The Radiant. It is where the Deluna Dukedom lies.

  • Capital: Justicarna
  • Current Ruler: Darhak Mermin Justicar
  • Known for: Holy Knightly Orders, Sprawling Agricultural Regions and Sparse Monster Forests

The Chronomear Theocracy

Led by the High Pope

A religious state governed by the High Priests of the Old Gods. Here, faith is law and magic is both blessing and curse depending on one's devotion. Peasents are said to have the easist live here as theocracy's main goal is to keep them happy. The prayers of those who are safe and without stress is what their god Chronomear desires. The church is also well known for picking up any orphans they find. If they wild magic, theyeither become border gaurding magesor with enough talent, the they become high clerics. Those without magic talent become knights.

  • Capital: Holy City Chronois
  • Ruler: Holy Pope Elara Chronos
  • Known for: Religious Temples Abundant, Holy Artifacts, Constant Prayers, Powerful Clerics

The Ouroaeth Empire

Led by the Sisinius Imperial Family

A vast military power that has conquered much of the eastern side of the Morgath Continet through superior strategy and technological advancement. Their legion of vampires are ruthless but efficient. Their northern region extends all the way into the Basker Continet where their main foes reside. Cureently,they are facing major wars to the smaller kingdoms and independent lands to their east, preventing them from key port locations.

  • Capital: Snarmain
  • Emperor: Emperor Magnus III
  • Known for: Military Might, Engineering. Vampire Legions, Vineyards
